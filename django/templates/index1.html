About Me

My name is Wensly Rajkumar, I am passionate about modern DevOps practices in designing resilient cloud infrastructure, automating deployments, improving platform reliability, and enabling engineering teams to deliver software faster through Infrastructure as Code.

My Story

I was born and raised in Chennai, one of South India's most vibrant and culturally rich cities. I completed my Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) from the University of Madras in 2012 , Soon after graduation, I joined Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) as a fresher, where I spent more than a decade (2012–2023)
Today I am Looking back, I see my journey at TCS as horizontal growth instead of specializing particular management skillset. My first project as an Information Processing Specialist supporting Citibank N.A. This role laid the foundation for my understanding of enterprise operations and the importance of accuracy, process discipline, willingness to embrace new challenges, I transitioned into a Fraud Analyst role supporting payment card services. This was a completely new domain for me, requiring strong analytical thinking, attention to detail, reasoning skills

As my experience grew, I was promoted to Process Lead, where I managed the Configuration Management Database (CMDB). I took ownership of maintaining data integrity, relationship mapping, audit readiness, More importantly, this role allowed me to collaborate closely with infrastructure, application, and operations teams across multiple business functions. This proved to be the defining turning point in my career. Working as a CMDB Administrator exposed me to the broader ecosystem of enterprise IT and opened the door to SRE and DevOps with has continued at my second company as well SLK Software Pvt Ltd now “Altimetrik”

Today, as a Senior DevOps|SRE Engineer, I combine my operational background with expertise in cloud technologies, Infrastructure as Code, Kubernetes, CI/CD, observability, automation, and Site Reliability Engineering. Every role I have held has contributed to the engineer I am today, reinforcing the belief that diverse experiences often create the strongest technical leaders.

This website is a reflection of that journey—my experiences, the technologies I am passionate about, the projects I have built, and my commitment to continuous learning. As technology continues to evolve, I remain committed to growing alongside it, embracing every opportunity to innovate, automate, and build reliable platforms that create lasting value.
